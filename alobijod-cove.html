Alobijod Cove

Enjoy the coastal scenery of Guimaras and discover nearby islands through locally arranged excursions.

Destination Overview

About Alobijod Cove

Alobijod Cove is a coastal destination in the municipality of Nueva Valencia, Guimaras. Its shoreline is a starting point for visitors exploring the island's coastal attractions and nearby islets. Visitors can arrange local boat excursions where available, but the itinerary, access, and activities depend on local operators and weather conditions.

Location

Alobijod Cove, Nueva Valencia, Guimaras, Philippines.

Highlights

Coastal views, nearby island excursions, and access to Guimaras's seaside attractions.

Best For

Relaxing by the coast, island-hopping trips, photography, and exploring Guimaras.

Visitor Guide

Things to Do

  • Enjoy the coastal scenery around Alobijod.
  • Ask local operators about available island-hopping trips.
  • Explore nearby islets when boat access and conditions permit.
  • Take photographs of the coastline and surrounding waters.
  • Discover other local attractions as part of a planned Guimaras visit.
Responsible Tourism

Before You Visit

  • Confirm boat availability, departure times, and tour inclusions.
  • Check weather and sea conditions before departing.
  • Follow boat operator safety instructions.
  • Keep beaches clean and avoid disturbing marine life.
  • Bring drinking water, sun protection, and suitable footwear.
