Alona Beach, Panglao

Discover a tropical beach known for its clear waters, beachfront restaurants, island-hopping trips, and easy access to the beautiful waters of Bohol.

Destination Overview

About Alona Beach

Alona Beach is a popular coastal destination on Panglao Island in Bohol. Its white-sand shoreline, clear water, resorts, restaurants, and diving centers attract visitors looking for both relaxation and outdoor activities. It is also a common starting point for island-hopping trips around nearby marine attractions. Conditions and activities may vary depending on the weather and season.

Location

Alona Beach, Panglao Island, Province of Bohol, Central Visayas, Philippines.

Highlights

White sand, clear water, beachfront dining, diving centers, and island-hopping excursions.

Best For

Beach relaxation, swimming, snorkeling, diving, couples, and family holidays.

Visitor Guide

Things to Do

  • Relax on the beach and enjoy the coastal views.
  • Visit the restaurants and cafés along the shoreline.
  • Arrange an island-hopping or snorkeling trip.
  • Explore diving opportunities with a qualified operator.
  • Enjoy the beach during the late afternoon and sunset.
Responsible Tourism

Before You Visit

  • Check the weather and sea conditions before water activities.
  • Use reef-safe practices and avoid damaging coral.
  • Follow guides' safety instructions during excursions.
  • Do not touch or disturb marine wildlife.
  • Keep the beach clean and respect local communities.
