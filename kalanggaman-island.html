Kalanggaman Island

Discover a stunning white sandbar, turquoise waters, and the peaceful natural scenery of Palompon, Leyte.

Destination Overview

About Kalanggaman Island

Kalanggaman Island is a small island in Palompon, Leyte, famous for its long white sandbar and clear turquoise waters. Its beautiful coastline makes it a popular destination for swimming, snorkeling, photography, and day trips. The island has limited facilities and a relatively undeveloped environment, so visitors should prepare their supplies before traveling. Its natural surroundings are an important part of its appeal, making responsible tourism essential to preserving the island.

Location

Kalanggaman Island, Municipality of Palompon, Province of Leyte, Philippines.

Highlights

Long white sandbar, turquoise water, island scenery, swimming, and snorkeling.

Best For

Beach relaxation, photography, swimming, snorkeling, and nature-focused island trips.

Visitor Guide

Things to Do

  • Walk along the island's famous white sandbar.
  • Enjoy the sea views and take photos of the coastline.
  • Swim in designated areas when sea conditions are safe.
  • Explore the surrounding waters through permitted snorkeling activities.
  • Ask local operators about available island activities and guided trips.
Responsible Tourism

Before You Visit

  • Arrange boat transfers and check visitor requirements in advance.
  • Bring drinking water, sun protection, and personal essentials.
  • Follow local rules regarding swimming and protected areas.
  • Never touch, chase, or disturb marine wildlife.
  • Take your trash with you and avoid removing sand, shells, or coral.
