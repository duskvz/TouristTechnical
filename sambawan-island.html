Sambawan Island

Explore green hills, rocky islets, and clear blue waters surrounding one of Biliran's scenic island destinations.

Destination Overview

About Sambawan Island

Sambawan Island is located off the coast of Maripipi, Biliran. It is known for its grassy hills, rocky formations, and clear waters that surround its small group of islets. Visitors can climb to elevated viewpoints to enjoy views of the sea and nearby islands. The area is also known for snorkeling and diving opportunities, with marine life making the surrounding waters attractive to nature lovers. Visitors should plan their transfers and activities ahead of time because facilities and services may be limited.

Location

Sambawan Island, Municipality of Maripipi, Province of Biliran, Philippines.

Highlights

Grassy hills, scenic viewpoints, rocky islets, snorkeling, diving, and island scenery.

Best For

Nature lovers, outdoor adventures, snorkeling, photography, and scenic island exploration.

Visitor Guide

Things to Do

  • Climb to the island's elevated viewpoints for panoramic sea views.
  • Explore the coastline and enjoy the surrounding scenery.
  • Go snorkeling in areas approved by local guides.
  • Ask qualified local operators about available diving activities.
  • Check whether camping is permitted and available during your visit.
Responsible Tourism

Before You Visit

  • Arrange boat transfers and confirm local visitor requirements in advance.
  • Wear suitable footwear for uneven paths and grassy slopes.
  • Follow marine sanctuary rules and local guide instructions.
  • Never touch coral or disturb marine wildlife.
  • Take your trash with you and respect the island's natural environment.
