Manjuyod Sandbar

Experience a stretch of white sand surrounded by shallow turquoise water, with scenic views and boat trips along the coast of Negros Oriental.

Destination Overview

About Manjuyod Sandbar

Manjuyod Sandbar is a well-known coastal attraction near Bais City in Negros Oriental. Often called the Maldives of the Philippines, it is known for its white sand, shallow clear waters, and cottages built on stilts above the sea. The sandbar is most visible during low tide, so the experience depends on the tide schedule and weather conditions. Boat excursions may also include dolphin watching in nearby waters, although sightings are not guaranteed.

Location

Manjuyod Sandbar, near Bais City, Province of Negros Oriental, Philippines.

Highlights

White sand, shallow turquoise water, stilt cottages, boat excursions, and coastal scenery.

Best For

Photography, sightseeing, families, couples, and travelers who enjoy boat trips and nature.

Visitor Guide

Things to Do

  • Walk along the sandbar when it is exposed at low tide.
  • Take photographs of the sandbar and surrounding sea.
  • Arrange a boat excursion with a local operator.
  • Enjoy the views of the stilt cottages over the water.
  • Ask local guides about responsible dolphin-watching trips.
Responsible Tourism

Before You Visit

  • Check tide schedules and weather conditions before traveling.
  • Bring sun protection and drinking water.
  • Follow the boat operator's safety instructions.
  • Do not chase, touch, or feed dolphins and other wildlife.
  • Take your rubbish with you and respect the marine environment.
