Calicoan Island, ABCD Beach

Discover Pacific Ocean surf breaks, rugged coastlines, and the laid-back atmosphere of Guiuan, Eastern Samar.

Destination Overview

About Calicoan Island

Calicoan Island is located in Guiuan, Eastern Samar, along the Pacific coast of the Philippines. It is known for its surfing areas, including ABCD Beach, which attracts surfers looking for ocean waves and a coastal adventure. Beyond surfing, visitors can appreciate the island's natural scenery, explore coastal areas, and discover tidal pools when conditions allow. The island has a relaxed atmosphere that appeals to travelers who enjoy outdoor activities. Since the coast faces the Pacific Ocean, visitors should always check sea conditions and follow local safety advice.

Location

Calicoan Island, Municipality of Guiuan, Province of Eastern Samar, Philippines.

Highlights

Surfing, Pacific Ocean views, coastal landscapes, tidal pools, and nearby natural attractions.

Best For

Surfers, outdoor adventurers, photographers, and travelers seeking a relaxed coastal escape.

Visitor Guide

Things to Do

  • Visit ABCD Beach and observe the local surfing scene.
  • Arrange surfing lessons with a qualified local instructor.
  • Enjoy the Pacific Ocean views and photograph the coastline.
  • Explore tidal pools only when the tide and sea conditions are safe.
  • Ask local guides about nearby coastal attractions and caves.
Responsible Tourism

Before You Visit

  • Check weather forecasts, surf conditions, and local travel advisories.
  • Follow the advice of local surfers, guides, and safety personnel.
  • Do not enter rough water or explore tidal pools during dangerous conditions.
  • Keep the coastline clean and avoid damaging natural features.
  • Respect local residents, fishing areas, and private property.
