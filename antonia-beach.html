Antonia Beach

Discover white sand, turquoise waters, and dramatic limestone formations in the Gigantes Islands of northern Iloilo.

Destination Overview

About Antonia Beach

Antonia Beach is a coastal destination in the Gigantes Islands, part of the municipality of Carles in northern Iloilo. Visitors come for its sandy shoreline, clear waters, and surrounding limestone scenery. It is commonly included in island-hopping trips around the Gigantes group. Sea conditions and available activities can vary with the weather.

Location

Antonia Beach, Gigantes Islands, Carles, Iloilo, Philippines.

Highlights

Sandy shores, clear coastal waters, limestone scenery, and island-hopping opportunities.

Best For

Beach relaxation, photography, swimming when conditions are safe, and island exploration.

Visitor Guide

Things to Do

  • Enjoy the beach and surrounding island scenery.
  • Take photographs of the shoreline and rock formations.
  • Explore nearby islands through a locally arranged island-hopping trip.
  • Swim only in permitted areas when sea conditions are safe.
  • Ask local operators about available activities and schedules.
Responsible Tourism

Before You Visit

  • Check sea and weather conditions before travelling.
  • Follow boat operator instructions and wear provided safety equipment.
  • Bring drinking water and sun protection.
  • Take your rubbish with you and avoid disturbing marine life.
  • Confirm transport, tour inclusions, and local requirements in advance.
