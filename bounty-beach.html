Bounty Beach, Malapascua

Discover a peaceful island shoreline, relaxed beachfront dining, and access to some of the Philippines' most well-known diving experiences.

Destination Overview

About Bounty Beach

Bounty Beach is a popular beachfront area on Malapascua Island in Daanbantayan, northern Cebu. It is known for its sandy shoreline, clear coastal waters, small resorts, restaurants, and dive shops. The beach provides a convenient base for visitors who want to relax by the sea or explore the waters surrounding the island. Malapascua is also famous for thresher shark diving excursions, particularly around Monad Shoal, although wildlife sightings can never be guaranteed.

Location

Bounty Beach, Malapascua Island, Municipality of Daanbantayan, Province of Cebu, Philippines.

Highlights

Sandy shores, beachfront restaurants, diving excursions, island scenery, and relaxed seaside stays.

Best For

Beach relaxation, scuba diving, photography, couples, and travelers looking for an island escape.

Visitor Guide

Things to Do

  • Relax along the sandy shoreline and enjoy the sea views.
  • Visit local restaurants and beachfront cafés.
  • Arrange a guided diving excursion with a qualified operator.
  • Explore nearby beaches and island attractions.
  • Ask local operators about thresher shark diving trips.
Responsible Tourism

Before You Visit

  • Arrange boat transfers to Malapascua in advance.
  • Check sea conditions and local travel advisories.
  • Follow your dive guide's safety instructions.
  • Never touch, chase, or disturb marine wildlife.
  • Dispose of waste properly and respect the local community.
