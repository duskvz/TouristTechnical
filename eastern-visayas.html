Discover remote island escapes, marine sanctuaries, unusual coastal landscapes, and unforgettable ocean views across Leyte, Biliran, Northern Samar, and Eastern Samar.

Kalanggaman Island

Palompon, Leyte

Famous for its long, bone-white sandbar and crystal-clear waters, this protected island offers a peaceful escape for swimming, snorkeling, photography, and enjoying unspoiled coastal scenery.

Sambawan Island

Maripipi, Biliran

A striking island marine sanctuary featuring grassy hills, rocky coastlines, and clear water. Hike to an elevated viewpoint, snorkel around the island, or enjoy a quiet night camping.

Explore Destination

Sila Island Pink Beach

San Vicente, Northern Samar

A rare coastal destination known for its pink-tinted sand, formed by a mixture of pale beach sand and reddish coral fragments. Its remote setting makes it appealing to travelers seeking a quieter island experience.

Explore Destination

Calicoan Island / ABCD Beach

Guiuan, Eastern Samar

Known for its Pacific-facing coastline and surf breaks, Calicoan Island attracts surfers and adventurous travelers. Explore its coastal scenery, tidal pools, and nearby natural attractions while respecting local conditions.
